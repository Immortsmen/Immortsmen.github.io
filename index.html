Research

My current interests focus on persistent AI agents, self-evolving systems, multimodal reasoning, and intelligent systems that can operate over long horizons.

Agent Memory & Self-Evolution

Exploring how autonomous agents can learn from previous tasks through long-term memory, experience retrieval, reflection, skill evolution, and recursive self-improvement.

Multimodal Agent Reasoning

Studying how agents integrate visual, spatial, and structured information to perform complex reasoning and decision making. My research experience includes multimodal geo-economic reasoning involving satellite imagery, POI data, and population information.

Multi-Agent & Organizational Memory

Exploring how multiple persistent agents share knowledge, coordinate tasks, maintain memory, and collaborate across long-running workflows.

Computer Vision

Research experience in computer vision, visual representation, and feature learning, with an interest in connecting visual perception with multimodal agents and intelligent systems.

Building

Beyond research, I am interested in turning agent systems into practical AI products, digital workers, and AI-native organizations.

Digital Employees

Exploring AI systems that combine planning, memory, retrieval, tool use, and execution to perform real workflows instead of only generating answers.

AI-native Organizations

I am interested in how small teams, and potentially individual founders, can use persistent AI agents to build increasingly capable digital organizations.

AI Entrepreneurship

Building and testing AI products through research, hackathons, and entrepreneurial programs, with a focus on translating new agent capabilities into practical products.
